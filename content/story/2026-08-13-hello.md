Google Flights is a timetable. Kayak is a grid. We wanted something that actually **hunts** — flexible origins, a continent instead of a city, a price ceiling, a date window, and an opportunity to discover new possibilities.

That is live today on [findcheapflights.ai](/).

## What you can do now

- Hunt from airports near you to a whole continent, or lock a destination and let origins flex within about six hours of home.
- Round trip or one way, with a stay length, a date window, and optional min/max price.
- Theme a hunt so the fare matches a trip you would actually take; clubs, beaches, ski, food, Christmas markets, and the rest.
- One free live hunt. Then [Pro](/pricing) for $11.99/month or $119.99/year and 100 hunts a month.
- Reopen past reports in History. We find the fare; you book it on the airline or OTA yourself.

This page is the running story. New dated entries land here from our developers as the product changes. Stay tuned!
