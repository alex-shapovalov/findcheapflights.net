We will add a short onboarding so we can learn what you actually like — beaches, cities, food, ski, nightlife — and tailor the flights we hunt to your interests.

Save a hunt and we email you when new cheap inventory shows up, weekly or every other week. You do not have to come back to check. We will also send you similar cheap flights you may be interested in.

We want to learn from you and give you a custom personalized blog to help you find the cheap destinations we know you'd love.

## Continuous improvement

We hope to deliver travel tips and continuous reinvestment into the platform as we grow, especially on efficiency and speed.
